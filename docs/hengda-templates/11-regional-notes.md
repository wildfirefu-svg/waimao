# Regional Notes

**USA:** Use accurate sender information, a clear subject line, company address, and an unsubscribe option. Do not use misleading claims.

**Europe:** Be more selective. Prefer company-level or role-based emails found from public business sources. Keep source records and avoid repeated outreach if there is no response.

**Middle East:** Email can be used, but relationship building and follow-up through trade platforms, exhibitions, and messaging channels may be important.

**Southeast Asia:** Keep emails short and practical. Focus on price, samples, lead time, and suitable applications after the customer replies.
